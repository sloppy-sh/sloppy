## What it is for

How a project keeps one account of why it is the way it is, and how people and agents write into it. The notes are a field both of them move around in: each a decision placed under what it sprang out of, tagged with its system, and pointing at the code it explains. The rule that the notes come first, so the code answers to them, is held in [Intent-driven development: the note comes first](sloppy:did:syr:z6MkofhdxKmE23ZUPQ65Lj7SynGSbLsL1ZNc81tgqQhg8h6X/01M5RYFJCQCRP07NJ8P1A8N5W5).
