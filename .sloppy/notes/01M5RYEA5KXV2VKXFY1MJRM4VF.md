## What it is for

The feeling every surface is held to. Plain type, generous space, a canvas that stays still unless somebody moves it, and surfaces almost without colour: neutrals keep their chroma at or under 0.02, so the ground shifts no hue the graph draws on it. Nothing congratulates, nothing nags — no streaks, no counts, no manufactured urgency. A person can put a half-formed thought down without tidying it first and rely on the app saying nothing about it.

The floors that keep the quiet legible are measured rather than assumed: [token-contrast.test.ts](code:packages/ts/ui/src/lib/token-contrast.test.ts) reads the stylesheet and holds every look to them.

## Why

DESIGN.md § "The feeling": "Nothing congratulates you, nothing nags. The chrome is a margin; the graph is the page." The opening of [DESIGN.md](code:DESIGN.md) gives the reason for the near-colourless ground: "almost colourless, so that the colour the graph does spend means something." AI.md § "Design & Product": "a thinking tool does not nag."

PRODUCT.md § "Brand Personality" names the promise: "the input is allowed to be sloppy, because the structure is what is rigorous", and "A good notebook does not have opinions about your handwriting."

## Decided against

PRODUCT.md § "Anti-references" names what this is not: engagement metrics on thinking — "anything that makes a half-formed idea feel like a post that underperformed" — productivity-guru PKM that implies "you are behind on your own thoughts", and "AI-made" sameness: "purple gradients, glassmorphism for its own sake, chrome that shows off instead of serving."
