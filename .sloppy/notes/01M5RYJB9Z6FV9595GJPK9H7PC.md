## What it is for

The product is called Sloppy — [productName and the sh.sloppy.app identifier](code:apps/sloppy/native/src-tauri/tauri.conf.json#productName) carry it into the app — and the name is a promise about who it is for and how it speaks. The input may be sloppy because the structure is what is rigorous: a person puts a half-formed thought down without a title, a folder, a tag or a number, and the protocol supplies the shape. The tone follows: quiet, exacting, dry rather than warm, plain about what happened. It is for one person thinking, the people they let read and write beside them, and the developer and the agent documenting beside them ([PRODUCT.md](code:PRODUCT.md) § "Users").

<!-- block 01M5RYJBA1VYCE6JCCGE35DY9V -->

## Why

The developer's r/ContextEngineering post "Sloppy - Context/Drift-avoidance for humans and agents": "As for the name, I thought it would be funny, Sloppy, the tool which can help you write non-sloppy code." [PRODUCT.md](code:PRODUCT.md) § "Brand Personality": "The name is the promise: the input is allowed to be sloppy, because the structure is what is rigorous." and "A good notebook does not have opinions about your handwriting." § "Product Purpose": "It is a thinking tool that happens to be social, not a social network that happens to hold notes."

<!-- block 01M5RYJBA33MZNW1KS89JZYN38 -->

## Decided against

[PRODUCT.md](code:PRODUCT.md) § "Anti-references": "Second brain" productivity-guru PKM, "systems sold as discipline"; social feeds and engagement metrics on thinking, "anything that makes a half-formed idea feel like a post that underperformed"; and "AI-made" sameness, "chrome that shows off instead of serving." The two anti-references about structure are in [The third position: a graph whose shape is agreed](sloppy:did:syr:z6MkofhdxKmE23ZUPQ65Lj7SynGSbLsL1ZNc81tgqQhg8h6X/01M5RYEB9587Z0AXMGRT0CTG47).
