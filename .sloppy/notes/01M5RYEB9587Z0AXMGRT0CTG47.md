## What it is for

Sloppy stands between two ways of keeping notes. One imposes a tree before the thought exists; the other imposes nothing, and leaves a mesh of backlinks whose picture means something only to its owner. Sloppy's position is that **the shape of the graph is part of the protocol**: where a note stands follows from what it sprang out of and from rules every peer applies the same way, so one person's graph draws the same shape on somebody else's screen. [The graph reading](code:docs/images/reading-the-graph.jpg) is that shape drawn. The rules themselves are [The protocol: what every peer reads the same way](sloppy:did:syr:z6MkofhdxKmE23ZUPQ65Lj7SynGSbLsL1ZNc81tgqQhg8h6X/01M5RY52DDY8ADPXG2ZWPG96PN).

What a person can rely on: a graph somebody else wrote can be read, pulled and reasoned about without its author there to explain it, and that agreement is what makes the graph social rather than merely shared.

<!-- block 01M5RYEB99N24W75AND8VKF11B -->

## Why

[AI.md](code:AI.md) § "Project" names the failure on both sides: "Notion imposes a tree: pages inside pages, structure decided before the thought exists. Obsidian imposes nothing: a flat mesh of backlinks where the graph view is decorative, because no two people's graphs mean the same thing." [PRODUCT.md](code:PRODUCT.md) § "Product Purpose" states the aim it serves: a body of thought whose "shape survives being read by somebody else".

<!-- block 01M5RYEB9B8G9MDC87JH6RDSEV -->

## Decided against

[PRODUCT.md](code:PRODUCT.md) § "Anti-references" rejects both ends by name: "Notion-shaped page trees — a hierarchy you must commit to before the thought exists", and "Obsidian's graph view as decoration — a pretty hairball that means nothing to anyone but its owner, because no two people's graphs share a shape." In the r/ContextEngineering post "Sloppy - Context/Drift-avoidance for humans and agents", the developer also set aside "just building a notion/obsidian plugin" in favour of a separate approach.
