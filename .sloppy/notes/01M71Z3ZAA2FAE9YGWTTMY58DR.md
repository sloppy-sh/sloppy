## What it is for

A condition `harness` in [conditions.json](code:bench/conditions.json): the same task packs, the same blind judge, the same lanes, with the runner composing the window and driving the model. It is reported as a fourth bar beside the three in [bench/README.md](code:bench/README.md) § "The comparison that is reported" — documents alone, subagents, Sloppy as served today — on the same shaped documents as Sloppy's bar, so the only change from the third bar to the fourth is who composes the window. It runs first on Milvus and GitLab, where finding is the binding constraint and the margins are largest, and its prediction is written into [bench/SURVEY.md](code:bench/SURVEY.md) before it runs, as every result there is.

<!-- block 01M71Z3ZAB433TJAFYXQ98ZDXW -->

## Why

- [bench/SURVEY.md](code:bench/SURVEY.md) § "Where the gain is": \*brief\* at −0.042 is the family a composed window is for. If the fourth bar does not move \*brief\*, the window is not doing what the note above says it does.
- The `api` backend already exists in the bench, so the condition is wiring; and the bench's rule that a result stands whichever way it goes is what lets the word "harness" be earned by a number rather than claimed.

<!-- block 01M71Z3ZAB9WZE84P7M0AQCYC1 -->

## What would show it wrong

- The fourth bar at or below the third on \*brief\* and \*why\*: composing is no better than fishing, and the loop earns nothing on its own.
- The fourth bar ahead only on cost: the window saves tokens without changing what the model gets right, which is worth having and is not a harness's claim.
